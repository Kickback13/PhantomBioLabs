Our process

From synthesis to shipment.

Five checkpoints between a raw material order and a vial on your bench.

  1. 01

    Synthesis

    Custom solid-phase synthesis at a certified partner facility. Every batch traced to raw material origin.

  2. 02

    Purification

    Reverse-phase HPLC to 99% purity. Retention data logged and archived alongside the lot.

  3. 03

    ISL Labs verification

    ISL Labs independently confirms mass, purity, and identity. Their Certificate of Analysis is what ships with your order.

  4. 04

    Lyophilization

    Vacuum-dried under sterile conditions. Sealed, labeled, and QC'd for weight uniformity.

  5. 05

    Same-day fulfilment

    Discreet, temperature-monitored packaging. Orders placed before 2 pm ET dispatch the same business day.

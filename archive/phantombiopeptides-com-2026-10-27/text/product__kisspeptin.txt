Kisspeptin is a signaling peptide studied in the hormonal category for its central role in initiating and regulating the reproductive hormone cascade. Research has examined its influence on the release of GnRH, LH, and downstream sex hormones, as well as its role in fertility and puberty. This 10 mg vial supports investigators studying reproductive endocrinology and the HPG axis. It is supplied as a lyophilized powder for reconstitution with bacteriostatic water. Sold for laboratory research use only, not for human consumption.

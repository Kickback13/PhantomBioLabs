Bacteriostatic water (Recon Water) is the standard reconstitution solvent used to prepare freeze-dried peptides for research. This 3 mL vial contains sterile water with 0.9% benzyl alcohol, a preservative that inhibits bacterial growth so a reconstituted vial can be accessed multiple times over an extended period. It's an essential companion supply for any peptide research workflow, keeping prepared solutions stable and contamination-free. The compact 3 mL size is ideal for smaller peptide vials or single-project use. Sold as a laboratory supply for research and reconstitution purposes only.

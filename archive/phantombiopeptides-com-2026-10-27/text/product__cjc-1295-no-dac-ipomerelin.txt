This popular combination pairs CJC-1295 with Ipamorelin in a single 10 mg vial, blending two peptides frequently studied together in the growth-hormone category. CJC-1295 is a growth-hormone-releasing hormone analog, while Ipamorelin is a selective secretagogue; together they are researched for their potential to stimulate a stronger, more sustained pulse of growth hormone. The pairing is valued in research for combining a steady baseline with a clean, targeted release. Co-formulating them saves preparation time and keeps ratios consistent across a study. Supplied as a freeze-dried powder for research use only, not for human consumption.

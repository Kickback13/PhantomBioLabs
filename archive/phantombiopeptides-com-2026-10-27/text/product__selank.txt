Selank is a synthetic peptide studied in the cognitive category for its potential anxiolytic (anti-anxiety) and nootropic properties. Research has explored its influence on mood, stress resilience, and cognitive performance, partly through effects on brain-derived neurotrophic factor. This 5 mg vial gives researchers a supply for studies of anxiety pathways and cognitive support. It is delivered as a lyophilized powder for reconstitution with bacteriostatic water. Provided for laboratory research use only, not for human consumption.

Research explores its potential role in cellular growth, muscle-tissue development, and the downstream effects of the GH/IGF-1 axis. This 1 mg vial is a concentrated, research-grade supply for advanced growth-factor studies. It is freeze-dried for stability and reconstituted with bacteriostatic water prior to use.

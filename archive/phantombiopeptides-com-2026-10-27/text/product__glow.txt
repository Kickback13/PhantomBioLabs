This trio is studied together for potential synergistic effects on tissue repair, skin quality, and overall regeneration - the inspiration behind the 'GLOW' name. It's a convenient all-in-one option for researchers who would otherwise reconstitute three separate peptides. The high total content supports extended or multi-arm study designs. Provided as a lyophilized powder for laboratory research use only, not for human consumption.

Tesamorelin is a growth-hormone-releasing hormone (GHRH) analog studied in the growth-hormone category for its effect on stimulating natural GH production. Research has examined its influence on GH release and, notably, on the reduction of visceral adipose tissue in metabolic studies. This 5 mg vial provides a research-grade supply for investigators exploring the GH axis and body-composition pathways. It is supplied as a lyophilized powder for reconstitution with bacteriostatic water. Provided for laboratory research use only, not for human consumption.
